TAIPAN

Hong Kong, 1860. Buy low, sell high, and keep your guns loaded.

How to play
  • Click the screen, then type. Single-key answers need no Enter.
  • Amounts take a number, or A for all.
  • Trade opium, silk, arms and general cargo between seven ports. Hong Kong has your bank, warehouse and moneylender.
  • In a sea fight, press R run, F fight or T throw cargo at any time. Your order repeats until you change it, and hesitating lets the enemy fire.
  • Reach a net worth of one million and you can retire from Hong Kong.
A fan remake of a 1982 Apple II trading game, written from scratch in Python and run in your browser with Pyodide.
